Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

River Math does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What happens on your device

  • The app works entirely on your iPhone or iPad and makes no network requests.
  • There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • Drill scores are kept in memory only while the app is open. They are not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Cards and amounts you enter in the Advisor are used only for the calculation on your device and are discarded.

Children

River Math does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
