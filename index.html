What River Math does

  • Practice: four drills on counting outs, turning outs into equity, pot odds and hand combos, each with a worked answer.
  • Advisor: enter your cards, the board, the pot and the bet you face to get a simulated equity and a bet, call, raise, check or fold suggestion.
  • Chart: reference tables for common draws, outs to equity, and the equity needed to call each bet size.

Common questions

Does River Math need an internet connection?

No. Everything runs on your device.

Can I play poker or bet real money in the app?

No. River Math is a study tool. There is no gameplay against other people and no way to place bets.

Why does the Advisor give slightly different numbers each time?

It estimates equity by simulating 20,000 random hands, so results can move by about half a percentage point between runs. The suggested action rarely changes.

How accurate is the Advisor?

It deals your opponents random hands from the range you choose. It can't read what their bets say about their cards, so after a big bet or raise, choose a tighter range.

Why did my drill score reset?

Scores are kept only while the app is open. Closing the app starts them fresh.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 15 or later.
